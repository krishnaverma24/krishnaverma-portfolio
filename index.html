About Me & Learning Journey

My Hardware Journey

Hello! I am Krishna Verma, an undergraduate student pursuing B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering at Pranveer Singh Institute of Technology (PSIT), Kanpur. As an enthusiastic learner in core electronics, I enjoy understanding how systems work from the ground up—starting from Boolean logic gates in VLSI to copper traces on a multi-layer PCB.

Through continuous self-learning, academic projects, and national competitions like NIDAR (Finalist — MeitY & Drone Federation of India) and Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2025, I am steadily building my foundation in RTL design, schematic capture, and microcontroller interfacing.

Quick Telemetry

  • Location: Kanpur City, UP
  • Degree: B.Tech ECE (CGPA 7.5)
  • Core Interest: VLSI, PCB & Embedded
  • Goal: Core Hardware Internships

What I Have Learned (Core Foundations Built)

  • Digital Electronics & Verilog Basics: Truth tables, K-map simplification, combinational/sequential blocks (ALU, registers, counters), and modular Verilog HDL.
  • PCB Schematic & Layout Design: Creating schematics, routing 4-layer boards, managing power/ground planes, DRC verification, and generating Gerber files in KiCad & EasyEDA.
  • Microcontroller & UAV Interfacing: Hands-on prototyping with ESP32, Arduino Nano, Raspberry Pi, NRF24L01 wireless modules, GPS, ESCs, and flight controllers.

Currently Learning & Exploring (Next Milestones)

  • 8-Bit Custom CPU Integration: Completing full datapath integration, custom ISA instruction decoding, and waveform testbench verification in Verilog.
  • FPGA & Semiconductor Flow: Deepening my understanding of RTL synthesis, CMOS digital IC fundamentals, and Static Timing Analysis (STA) concepts.
  • Embedded C++ & Signal Integrity: Strengthening bare-metal firmware practices and learning best practices for mixed-signal multi-layer PCB routing.
